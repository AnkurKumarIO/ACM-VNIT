Who We Are

As the official student chapter of ACM at VNIT Nagpur, we've been pushing boundaries since 2009. From R&D to open-source and technical events, we turn ideas into real-world impact.

We don't just write code — we ask questions, build with purpose, and learn together. With a culture built on trust and innovation, we grow as a community.

Institutional Foundation

VNIT Nagpur

Founded in 1960, Visvesvaraya National Institute of Technology is one of India's premier engineering institutions — renowned for academic excellence, cutting-edge research, and a vibrant campus culture.

Global Affiliation

ACM International

The world's largest computing society since 1947 — uniting educators, researchers, and professionals to advance computing as a science and a profession across 100K+ members globally.

01 / About

We don't just write code. We build with purpose.

As the official ACM student chapter at VNIT Nagpur, we've been pushing boundaries and challenging conventions since 2009. From research and development to open-source contributions and large-scale technical events, we turn ideas into real-world impact.

Institutional Foundation

Where we come from.

VNIT Nagpur

Founded in 1960, Visvesvaraya National Institute of Technology is one of India's premier engineering institutions — renowned for academic excellence, cutting-edge research, and a vibrant campus culture with students from across the nation.

NIT · Est. 1960

ACM International

The world's largest computing society since 1947 — uniting educators, researchers, and professionals to advance computing as a science and profession. A global network of 100,000+ members driving innovation.

Global · 100K+ Members
